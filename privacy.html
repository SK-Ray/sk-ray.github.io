Privacy Policy

Stinky Kitties Fishing & S.K. Studios, LLC · Effective November 22, 2026

Zero Personal Data Collection

S.K. Studios, LLC does not collect, store, track, or sell any personal data, device identifiers, location data, or usage analytics from Stinky Kitties Fishing. The app contains no third-party advertising networks and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Local Game Data

Your high scores, audio preference, and selected kitty crew remain saved locally on your device only.

Apple Services (In-App Purchases & Game Center)

Optional In-App Purchases (the Extra Kitties Pack) are processed directly by Apple through StoreKit, and optional high-score leaderboards use Apple Game Center. S.K. Studios, LLC does not receive your payment details or personal Apple Account credentials. Any information handled by those Apple features is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
