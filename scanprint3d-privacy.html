Privacy Policy

ScanPrint3D & S.K. Studios, LLC · Effective November 25, 2026

Zero Personal Data Collection

S.K. Studios, LLC does not collect, upload, store, track, or sell any personal data, camera images, LiDAR depth maps, 3D models, device identifiers, or usage analytics from ScanPrint3D. The app contains no third-party advertising networks and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

100% On-Device Camera & LiDAR Processing

ScanPrint3D uses your device's camera and LiDAR depth sensor solely during active scanning sessions to reconstruct 3D printable models locally on your iPhone or iPad. Temporary capture photos are automatically deleted after reconstruction finishes, and saved .STL, .3MF, and .USDZ files remain in your local app vault until you export, share, or delete them.

Apple StoreKit 2 In-App Purchases

Optional one-time In-App Purchases (Export Pro and Color Pro) are processed directly by Apple through StoreKit 2. S.K. Studios, LLC never receives your payment details or personal Apple Account credentials. Information handled by Apple is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
