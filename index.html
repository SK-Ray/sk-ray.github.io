Restoring Purchases (Extra Kitties Pack / Previous Remove Ads)

In Stinky Kitties Fishing 2.0, the app is completely ad-free and players who previously purchased Remove Ads receive the Extra Kitties Pack (6 extra kitties and vessels) at no extra cost.

  • Open Stinky Kitties Fishing and tap KITTY CREW on the title screen.
  • Tap RESTORE PURCHASES while signed into the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.
  • Your full 8-kitty crew unlocks immediately across both left and right boats.

Game Center Leaderboards

To save and compare high scores, make sure Game Center is enabled under iOS Settings > Game Center. Tap the trophy/bars button on the title screen or game-over screen to view standings.
