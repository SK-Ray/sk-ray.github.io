How to Play & Zoom

  • Tap any spotted difference on either photo to mark both panels together.
  • Pinch to zoom in for fine whisker or paw details and drag to pan around zoomed-in scenes.
  • Chapter 1 (Cafe Welcome, 9 puzzles) is completely free.

Restoring Purchases (Full Cafe Pass)

Full Cafe Pass is a one-time, family-shareable In-App Purchase ($0.99) that unlocks Chapters 2 & 3 (House Cats and Garden & Twilight, 18 additional puzzles) permanently with zero subscriptions or ads.

  • Open Cat Cafe and tap Browse Menu • 3 Chapters (or select House Cats / Garden & Twilight), or tap Full Cafe Pass on the home screen.
  • Tap Restore Previous Purchase while signed into the same Apple Account.

Game Center Leaderboards

Your total solved puzzles and star scores sync automatically to the Puzzles Solved leaderboard when signed into Game Center under iOS Settings > Game Center.
