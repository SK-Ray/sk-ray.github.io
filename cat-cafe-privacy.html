Privacy Policy

Cat Cafe: Spot the Difference & S.K. Studios, LLC · Effective November 23, 2026

Zero Personal Data Collection

S.K. Studios, LLC does not collect, store, track, or sell any personal data, device identifiers, location data, photos, or usage analytics from Cat Cafe: Spot the Difference. The app contains no third-party advertising networks and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Local Game Progress

Your solved puzzles, star ratings, best times, sound preferences, and chapter unlock status are stored locally on your device only via Apple UserDefaults and on-device StoreKit receipts.

Apple Services (In-App Purchases & Game Center)

The optional one-time In-App Purchase (Full Cafe Pass) is processed directly by Apple through StoreKit 2, and optional puzzle-solved leaderboards use Apple Game Center. S.K. Studios, LLC does not receive your payment details or personal Apple Account credentials. Information handled by those Apple features is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
