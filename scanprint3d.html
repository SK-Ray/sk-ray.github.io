1. Choosing the Right Scan Mode

Matching the capture mode to your object's real-world size makes the biggest difference in geometric sharpness and scale accuracy.

Mode Best Object Size How It Works Key Tips
MINI 3 – 8 cm (1 – 3 in)
Miniatures, lighters, figurines, game pieces, small hardware		 2 cm Ultra-Wide Macro lens + Vision subject isolation (no LiDAR depth) Hold 8–15 cm away so the item fills 60–80% of the reticle. Use SPIN or MANUAL tap. After building, tap SET MM with one real ruler/caliper dimension — macro photogrammetry is unscaled.
OBJECT 8 – 45 cm (3 – 18 in)
Mugs, statues, tools, cinder blocks, sneakers, everyday objects		 ARKit + LiDAR + HD Photogrammetry inside a 3D crop box Supports ORBIT (walk around), SPIN (lazy Susan), and + FLIP to stitch undersides. Use MINI for slender items under ~8 cm.
10FT • HD PHOTO 0.6 – 3.05 m (2 – 10 ft)
Chairs, sculptures, trash cans, furniture, large props		 3-band perimeter capture (LOW, MID, UP), up to 72 shots Best detail & photo texture for large objects. Spread 35–55 shots cleanly across all three height bands.
10FT • FAST LIDAR 0.6 – 3.05 m (2 – 10 ft)
Quick room props, plain furniture, fast size mockups		 Real-time LiDAR mesh reconstruction + RGB keyframe projection Builds in seconds with no photogrammetry wait. Best for quick dimensional mockups or low-texture surfaces.

2. Lighting & Backdrop Setup

  • Use bright, even, diffused light: Indirect daylight, soft room lighting, or two balanced lamps capture crisp surface detail without harsh shadows.
  • Avoid single-point spotlights or shifting shadows: Your own shadow crossing the object as you walk around interferes with feature matching.
  • Contrast your object against the table: Avoid scanning glossy or similarly colored items on bare polished wood. Place small items on a matte, contrasting cloth, felt pad, or plain slate coaster.
  • Elevate tiny flat-bottom objects slightly: Setting a tiny piece on a narrow matte riser (such as a dull bottle cap smaller than the base) keeps the table out of the silhouette so BASE CUT leaves a crisp footprint.
  • MINI Torch tip: Turn the Torch ON only for matte recessed textures in dim areas; keep it OFF on shiny plastics or metal so specular highlights do not slide across shots.

3. Difficult Materials & Practical Workarounds

Material / Shape Why Passive Scanning Struggles Practical Workaround
Glossy or solid-color plastic
(lighters, shiny toys, lacquered shells)		 Reflections shift every step while uniform plastic lacks trackable micro-texture. Use soft diffused lighting + MINI mode if <8 cm, or lightly dust with washable dry shampoo / cornstarch / talcum powder (or matte 3D scanning spray). Even a couple of tiny matte tape dots on large smooth faces help camera lock.
Bare chrome, polished metal, mirrors Mirrors reflect the surrounding room instead of showing their own surface geometry. Apply washable matte dulling spray/powder or matte painter's tape before capture and wipe clean afterward.
Open wire cages, grilles, fan blades Parallax seen through thin wire gaps causes mobile reconstruction to bridge across open grille faces. Photogrammetry works best on solid hulls; expect narrow grille gaps to bridge into a solid shell unless solid-backed or modeled cleanly afterward.
Clear glass & transparent acrylic Light refracts through the object into whatever sits behind it. Coat temporarily with washable matte spray/powder, or wrap in matte tape for reference measurements.
Best natural subjects Matte diffuse micro-texture locks thousands of sharp 3D points cleanly. Stone, concrete & cinder blocks, wood, ceramics, painted figures, clay, matte 3D prints, sneakers, fabric.

4. Capture Technique (ORBIT, SPIN, + FLIP)

  • Leave generous box headroom: After tapping your object, size the 3D box so the entire object — especially tops and handles — fits comfortably inside, and tap FLOOR to snap the green base plane to the table.
  • Step smoothly and pause briefly: Auto-shutter rejects motion blur automatically. Small pauses at each angle yield sharper geometry than continuous rapid movement.
  • Aim for the 30–48 shot sweet spot: Roughly 30–48 crisp, evenly spaced photos across multiple heights beat 70+ cluttered shots from one level (faster builds, cleaner detail). Tap DONE once coverage arcs are mostly filled.
  • Lazy Susan (SPIN): Keep the phone steady, turn the object ~15° at a time, and wait briefly for SNAP ✓. Finish Ring 1 (eye level), then raise the phone ~35–40° for Ring 2.
  • Stitching the bottom (+ FLIP): After Pass 1 has 10+ shots, tap + FLIP, rest the object on its side or upside down, place the Pass 2 crop box, and capture a second pass to seal the underside.

5. Inspection, Cleanup & Slicer Export

  1. Always check SOLID mode before exporting: Photo textures in COLOR can hide rough ripples or blobs. Tap SOLID to inspect the true uncolored watertight geometry, and check WIRE / X-RAY for mesh density.
  2. Level first with ORIENT: Use ORIENT → AUTO STAND or Pitch/Roll controls so the base sits level on the build plate before trimming.
  3. Trim a clean printing bottom with BASE CUT: Slide BASE CUT just high enough to slice away any residual tabletop skirt into a flat, sealed bottom. Toggle Smooth Rebuild only if you want Voxel Solidifier smoothing over a noisy surface.
  4. Set real millimeters with SCALE: Tap the dimension pill or SHARE → Scale / Resize Model… to enter one caliper/ruler width, depth, or height in mm (essential for MINI scans), or pick quick 50/100/150/200mm / 0.5× / 2× multipliers.
  5. Choose your export format:
    • .STL (Export Pro): Universal watertight binary STL for Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer, OrcaSlicer, Cura, Lychee, and Chitubox.
    • .3MF (Export Pro / Color Pro): Compact millimeter-scaled print package with optional vertex colors.
    • .USDZ (Color Pro): Full UV photo-textured 3D model for AR Quick Look and desktop 3D software.

6. Quick Troubleshooting

What Happened Likely Cause How to Fix It
Tiny object came out thick, blobby, or wood-colored Scanned <8 cm slender item in OBJECT mode on a wood/glossy desk Switch to MINI, set on a matte contrasting mat, fill most of the reticle, and calibrate via SET MM.
Top of model looked flat or sliced Box height was tight at the top edge Add a few cm of extra top clearance when sizing the box.
Shiny/chrome parts lost shape Moving reflections with no surface texture Use diffused lighting and washable matte powder/spray or small matte tape markers.
Processing took too long Excess redundant shots from one elevation Keep captures around 30–48 crisp shots across 2–3 height rings and tap DONE once coverage rings fill.
